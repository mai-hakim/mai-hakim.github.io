Live app

PaperShield

Photograph a letter. Get one clear answer: what to do, and by when.

Bills, insurance notices, government forms and scams all look official. PaperShield reads the letter on the phone and gives one of four answers. Each answer has a colour, an icon and a word, never colour alone. A fixed, published rule table decides, so the same letter always gets the same answer.

  • 🟢 No action · 🟠 Action needed · 🟣 Ask someone you trust (ready SMS / WhatsApp text) · 🔴 Signs of a scam (slow pulse, never flashing)
  • Letter summary, "See the letter", a magnifier you drag with your finger, A / A+, mute, and Listen on every screen
  • "I don't understand" tries a new way each time: simpler words, read slowly, the line on the letter, ask someone
  • Delete the letter (default) or save it on the phone only. No account, no upload, works offline

Tested (real runs): 59 of 59 automated checks passed at phone size in light and dark mode. axe-core found 0 WCAG A/AA violations on 13 screens. The 5 sample letters give the same answers as before. On 5 synthetic photos read on the device: 5 of 5 correct answers. Not yet tested with older adults.

A

Project A · n8n + Playwright + Gemini

Agentic App Tester

Agents explore, rules decide.

Four AI agents use a web app the way four people might: Rosa with low vision at 200% zoom, Walter with a hand tremor whose taps land a few pixels off, Grace using the app for the first time, and Harold, who is scared of scams. Each agent looks at the screen, decides where to tap, and makes a new plan when it gets stuck.

The agents only explore and describe. Every finding must be confirmed by a fixed check: axe-core, tap size, spacing, font size, zoom, broken links, console errors, or a tap that changed nothing. Fixed rules in n8n decide severity. Anything an agent "feels" but no check confirms is listed as unconfirmed, with no severity.

2 appstested: PaperShield and TodoMVC (a public demo app)

It found real bugs in my own app. A hand-tremor agent's shaky tap landed on "Scan a document" and exposed a crash that had been in PaperShield from the start. The agents also found "Read aloud" buttons with no visible feedback and a hidden skip link. Each one was confirmed by a fixed check, fixed, and gone in the next run. One finding is not fixed yet: a shaky tap on one sample button still missed in 1 of 2 runs. The tool also caught its own mistakes, 2 false errors and 2 checks that were too strict, and I fixed them before the final numbers.

Project B · n8n + Gemini

PaperShield letter workflow

AI reads the facts. Fixed rules decide. A person approves.

A letter comes in through an n8n form. The AI extracts facts only, and each fact comes with the exact quote it used, checked against the letter. The same rule table as the app decides the answer. Then a person approves or rejects before n8n makes a calendar reminder, writes the log sheet and emails the result. Failures go to an errors sheet. All data is synthetic.

Tested (real runs): 7 test cases: normal, missing data, wrong data, duplicate, AI unsure, AI tool down, and human rejects. Final run: 7 of 7 passed. Reading the emails also caught a real bug that the status checks missed ("Respond by null."). I fixed it and added a test for it.
